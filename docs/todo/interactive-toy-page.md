Three ideas so far:

- A DVD logo bouncing around with controls (size, speed, visuals) and a hit counter.
- A bouncy ball game where you launch a ball, Angry Birds style, to bounce off platforms into goal
  zones. This one could grow "items": portals, callbacks to other video games, and so on.
- Something in the spirit of _Shift_, the flash puzzle-platformer whose trick is inverting the level
  so the black background becomes the floor. Folded in here 2026-09-01 from its own backlog line,
